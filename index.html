HERD PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Herd (UK) is a daily yes/no polling app operated by RDIEM Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17309017), 13 Wricklemarsh Road, London SE3 0NF. RDIEM Ltd is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at info@bherd.uk.

In short: we share and sell anonymised poll results to third parties outside Herd, such as media organisations and researchers. We never share or sell your personal data, or anything that identifies you.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored securely, encrypted by our authentication provider).
  • Profile details you choose to give us: age range, gender, region, ethnicity, religion and sexuality. Each question includes a "Prefer not to say" option where relevant.
  • Your votes: your yes/no answer to each daily question.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, a device token so we can send the daily question reminder.

We do not collect your name, address, phone number, location, contacts or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

2. Why we use it

  • To run your account and let you vote once per question.
  • To show you, and others, how different groups voted.
  • To produce anonymised poll results that we share and sell to third parties (see section 4).
  • To send you the daily question notification, if you have allowed it.

3. Legal basis

We process your account details and votes to provide the service you sign up for (contract). Ethnicity, religion and sexuality are special category data under UK GDPR, so we only process them with your explicit consent, which you give when you complete your profile. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

4. Who we share and sell data to

We share and sell anonymised data to third parties outside Herd, such as:

  • media organisations, including publishers and broadcasters
  • researchers, including academic and market researchers

This data only shows totals and percentages for groups of people, for example "62% of women aged 25–34 in the North of England voted yes". We never share or sell individual vote records, and the data never includes your email address, account ID or anything that identifies you.

We also use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf, only to run the app:

  • Supabase: database and account login
  • Expo: delivering push notifications
  • Apple and Google: app distribution and notification delivery

These providers are not allowed to use your data for their own purposes. We never sell your personal data to anyone.

5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored by Supabase on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). The UK recognises the EU as providing adequate protection for personal data. Where any other provider processes data outside the UK or EU, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account, profile and votes until you delete your account. When you delete your account, your personal data is permanently removed. Anonymised results that have already been produced cannot be linked back to you and may be kept.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or by following the steps at our account deletion page. For any other request, email info@bherd.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Age

Herd is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
